## 3. Test Failure Analysis & Defect Summary

While all 45 automated pytest assertions passed, forensic analysis of the scenarios reveals critical **architectural limitations**:
1. **Scenario 1 & 3 (Face Liveness Deficit):** Scenario 1 passes as a software unit test *only* because the access policy is designed to require manual review for unvalidated faces. The AI capability itself (autonomous presentation attack rejection) is non-functional.
2. **Scenario 4 (Video Replay Deficit):** The system cannot autonomously distinguish a live person from a high-resolution video replay on a smartphone or tablet screen.
3. **Scenario 21 & 22 (VLM/NLP Heuristic Reliance):** Scenarios 21 and 22 pass by executing deterministic keyword parsers and string formatting templates, not neural language models.
